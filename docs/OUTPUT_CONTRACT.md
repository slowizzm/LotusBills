# Output Contract

Every completed review should use the smallest structure that covers the evidence.

## 1. Answer first

State the main conclusion in one to three sentences, including the most material issue or opportunity.

## 2. Coverage

List:

- Files reviewed.
- Billing periods covered.
- Currency and reporting date.
- Missing, unreadable, or conflicting sources.

## 3. Reconciliation and findings

Show only relevant checks. For each material finding include:

- Finding.
- Amount or effect.
- Evidence locator.
- Status: confirmed, likely, or needs verification when useful.
- Explanation or calculation.

Use a comparison table when reviewing three or more periods, bills, providers, or findings.

## 4. Recommendations

For each recommendation include:

- Priority.
- Proposed human action.
- Evidence.
- Estimated monthly and annual impact when calculable.
- Assumptions and uncertainty.
- Effort, tradeoffs, and risks.
- Required human verification.

## 5. Open items

End with the smallest set of decisions, documents, or questions needed from the user.

Never imply that a bill was paid, a provider was contacted, a service was changed, or a recommendation was executed.
